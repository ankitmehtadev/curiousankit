I have been using AI tools for a while now (obviously). I read about it, watch content related to it, and attend meetings and conferences where it is discussed. Every few days there is a new concept or technological advancement in the world of AI that shakes almost every industry and economy around the globe.

One fine day in the middle of August, I decided to pause and start learning the right way: from scratch.

I decided to create the Curious Ankit website for two reasons:

* I learn best when I have to explain what I did to someone else. So this site (or, rather, you, the reader) is the someone else.
* The teacher in me (the fourth generation in my family) and the mentor mindset in my blood love sharing knowledge and helping others.

Why the name "Curious Ankit", you ask? Thanks to my son's favourite fictional character, [Curious George](https://www.curiousgeorge.com). I also believe my two best qualities are curiosity to learn and the humility to accept what I don't know.

Over the coming months I will be working through AI the practical way: building small things, trying the tools properly, taking courses and writing down what I find. Some of it will be useful. Some of it will be a record of me getting things wrong, which is useful in its own way.
