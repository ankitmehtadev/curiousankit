## What you *won't* find here

* **Guides to making money with AI.** There are already plenty of YouTube videos and podcasts where people claim to have magically made thousands, even millions, of dollars in a week by building and selling something with AI. It might be true in some cases, and I am not denying that, but my journey is a bit different. My purpose is to learn to use AI to make my life easier, and to help someone who is on a similar journey.

* **The one perfect definition of AI, and of everything related to it.** The more I read about AI, the more I realise that different sources understand AI and its underlying concepts differently, and each of them seems to claim that it has the best definition. I am (or try to be) a very rational person. I read and listen to everyone's opinion on a topic, then come up with my own version of the definition. That makes it easier for me to learn, remember and explain concepts.

* **A claim that I am an AI genius.** I am not a genius in anything. I studied at Monash University and I loved its motto, "Ancora imparo", which translates to "I'm still learning". The (unverified) story behind this quote is that the great Renaissance artist Michelangelo said it at the age of 87, after he had painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling, sculpted the David and the Pietà, and designed the dome of St Peter's Basilica. I believe in the same principle. No matter how much knowledge I gain in a field, I have promised myself to always be a student.

* **An AI course.** I am not selling any AI or related courses. I teach at my alma mater and I am happy with that. This website is purely for me to document my journey with AI, and if it helps you in any way, it's my pleasure.

## What you *will* find here

* **Honesty.** Every experiment I run, and its record, will be documented here with complete honesty. If I fail at something, it will be noted with as much honesty as my successes. My notes and reflections are my honest opinions on a topic. You might or might not agree with me, and I totally respect that. I am always open to being corrected.

* **Human.** The thoughts, opinions and words are mine. I do use AI as a tool (to build this site, to check my grammar and to research), and I will always say so. If a typo survives, that's the human in the loop.

* **Trust.** I trust you to make your own decisions and not blindly follow me.

* **Improvement.** As I progress on this journey, you will notice some maturity in my content, thought process, experiments, experience and overall knowledge. If at any point you would like to help me get there faster, please feel free to reach out at [hello@curiousankit.com](mailto:hello@curiousankit.com) and share your journey.
